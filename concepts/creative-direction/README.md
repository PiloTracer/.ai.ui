# UIS-10 — Creative Direction

**Id:** UIS-10
**Directory:** `concepts/creative-direction/`
**Prompt:** [`prompt.md`](prompt.md)

## Trigger

Run UIS-10 when:

- Archetype is `marketing-site` or `hybrid` (marketing shell)
- Screen SPEC includes hero, landing page, or storytelling scroll
- Craft tier ≥ refined on any customer-facing marketing surface
- Agent generates a landing page or homepage layout

## Goal

Ensure marketing and creative surfaces are **visually distinctive, not just correct**. UIS-06 prevents generic AI chrome; UIS-10 pushes toward *memorable* design by evaluating typographic drama, visual tension, scroll narrative, and hero differentiation.

## Relationship to other concepts

| Concept | Role | UIS-10 adds |
|---------|------|-------------|
| UIS-01 | Hierarchy — one primary action | *Emotional* hierarchy — does the page feel intentional, not just organized? |
| UIS-06 | Anti-generic — flags AI slop | *Pro-distinction* — flags correct-but-boring |
| UIS-07 | Surface/control craft | Creative surfaces beyond cards/elevation — immersive heroes, editorial whitespace, typographic contrast |

**Run order:** UIS-10 before UIS-06. If UIS-10 passes but UIS-06 flags generic chrome, the creative intent should inform whether UIS-06 findings are real issues or deliberate choices documented in the SPEC.
