## Creative columns (UIS-10)

| id | heroPattern | mood | tensionTechnique | scrollRhythm |
|----|-------------|------|------------------|--------------|
| T1 | Illustrated narrative | Dark, technical, authoritative | Floating annotation cards on illustration create depth layers; orange CTA pops against dark | HIGH (hero) → LOW (logo trust row) → feature sections |
| T2 | Product-in-context | Professional, clear, functional | Device-framed screenshots prove the product exists; repeated CTA blocks create urgency | HIGH → MID → MID → HIGH — feature-heavy, needs breathing sections |
| T3 | Product-in-context | Demo-forward, confident | Live app preview in hero shows vs tells; tab interaction on marketing page is unusual and engaging | HIGH (app preview) → dense feature sections — benefits from LOW pause |
| T4 | *(app section)* | Organized, ecosystem-aware | Partner logo grid creates social proof through volume; status dots add real-time feel | App-like energy — marketplace browse pattern |
| T5 | *(content section)* | Transparent, developer-friendly | Timeline creates narrative arc of progress; version badges add visual anchors | Steady MID — reading/scanning mode |
| T6 | *(app section)* | Helpful, technical, precise | Sidebar + content split creates information architecture tension; code blocks add credibility | App-like — not scroll narrative |
| T7 | Editorial whitespace | Trustworthy, institutional | Trust badges (official icons) create instant credibility; accordion reveals depth on demand | LOW energy — builds trust through restraint, not excitement |
| T8 | *(comparison section)* | Confident, competitive | Feature matrix creates visual proof through pattern (checkmarks vs crosses); highlighted column draws eye | Decision-point energy — works best after value prop sections |
